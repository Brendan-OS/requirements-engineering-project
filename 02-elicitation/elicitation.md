## SH Needs and Concerns
Needs and concerns can overlap between users/Stakeholders.
- Finding and booking equipment
- Getting equipment when needed for teaching
- managing equipment and returns
- managing bookings and records
- control, availability and accountability
- Access to needed equipment and/or inacccess to needed equipment
- Equipment being stolen.
- Equipment not being taken with authorisation and then returned without alerting a member of staff in charge of the loaning of equipment
