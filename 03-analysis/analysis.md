## Observations
- The Current system is unreliable and difficult for people to understand.
- The People want a more efficient system.
- The Faculty want a more cost-effective solution.
- The IT and tech staff want less responsibility and burden that is current caused by the inefficiencies of the old system.

## Stakeholder Needs
- People are unsure if equipment is available.
- Lecturers want reliable availability information before promising equipment.

## Candidate Requirements
- Technicians need to know when equipment is due back.
- Students need to know if equipment required to pass a module will be available.
- If equipment is not available for said students' module - that there will be a system in place to prevent failure. 

## Solution Ideas
- The System shall record the expected return date for each booking.
- The College should build a mobile app.
- Pre-booking equipment.

## Assumptions
- User will have a college login.
- Everyone will use the new system that will be created.
- Stakeholders will not abuse the system.
