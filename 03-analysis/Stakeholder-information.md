## Who are the stakeholders?
- School Faclty
  - Lectuers
  - It
  - Technicians
  - Librarians
  - Resource Managers
- Students
  - Apprentices 
  - undergraduates 
  - post-graduates
  - Part-time

## What must the system do for them?
- Track who has what.
- Allow or disallow the loan of the equipment.
- Notify staff if equipment is lost or damaged.
- Allow the booking & pre-booking of equipment.

## Is there a deadline? (By start of next school year?)
- None specified. (unless you count the Req Eng module's project deadline - Mid Nov)

## why is a new system needed?
- The old system is outdated and inefficient.
  - There are too many methods of booking equipment.
  - The systems are unreliable in there methodology of tracking who has what equipment, and for how long.
  - Information is difficult to track down or keep.
  - Equipment has been reported missing and/or damaged.
- The proposed solutions will allow for more students to get the equipment they need.
  - On time.
  - A method of researching whether or not the college must purchase more equipment or not.
  - The one booking the equipment will be responsible for the equipment's state. (damaged or missing)

## How will we verify the needs?
- Inquiring with stakeholders.
- survey's. (Both in person and online)
