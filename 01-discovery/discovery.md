# Week 1 — Initial Discovery
Students and Staff want a method of accurately booking, with transparent methods that will allow equipment to be distributed to those who have inquired for them while also making it easier for students and staff to know who/where equipment is and when it will be avaiable for usage again.
## 1. Facts
1) The Current system replies on emails, paper and word of mouth. This multi-method of booking is inefficient.
2) The Students have reported double bookings. leaving them without equipment.
3) Staff have reported that they are still required to manually check for stock of certain equipment.
4) Staff have reported that equipment has not been returned at the time in which they had anticipated.
5) The multi-method of booking is causing confusion for all stakeholders, there are multiple reports of conflicting reports on scheduling and borrowing time.
## 2. Assumptions
1) I am assuming some equipment has been stolen and will not be returned, without the ability to track the equipment, the theif cannot be held accountable and may try to steal further equipment.
2) I assume staff do not always remember who and for how long, they allotted equipment to, and possibly why the equipment was alloted.
3) I assume students have little problem sharing the equipment with their close friends, possibly creating a mini-monopoly and leaving other students at a disadvantage.
4) Among the equipment listed some of it may be specifically for certain types of students, i.e. students with disabilities, or underprivileged students I assume this current system has caused them distress due to not being able to use accommodations they should be entitled to.
## 3. Unknowns
1) It is unknown if there is enough stock on equipment(s) to meet the demand(s).
2) If context/personal situations may affect equipment loans, i.e. someone without a laptop/PC being given priority for laptop equipment.
3) what calibre of solution is wanted. i.e. emails, paper or word of mouth. Possibly a fourth option such as a website or addition to a pre-existing app.

Make it easier to track the loan time of equipment for multiple stakeholders, but also for the saftey if the equipemnt.

## 7. Candidate Requirements
Functional;
1) Booking of equipment between xx/xx/xxxx until yy/yy/xxxx.
2) Knowledge for other stakeholders where the requested equipment is, if not available - when it is next available.
3) Pre-booking, as to allowing stakeholders to know that they will have the equipment at a certain time and until a certain time, before either time has arrived.
4) Protection, as tracked device are much less likely to go missing/be stolen/be damaged, as the stakeholder who'd booked the equipment will be held at fault.

Non-Functional;
1) The solution used must be capable and accurate while being used with an arbitrary number of students, as this is a college it can be assumed it would need to withstand well into the hundreds.
2) it is unlikely that equipment can always be available and/or in stock. therefore the method of booking must have a maximum booking date to accommodate cost vs effectiveness fir stakeholders.
